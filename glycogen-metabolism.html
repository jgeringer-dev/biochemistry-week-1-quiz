Final pathway challenge

Without looking at the answers, explain: glycogen → glucose-1-phosphate → glucose-6-phosphate, then compare the fate of glucose-6-phosphate in liver and skeletal muscle.

Model pathwayGlycogen phosphorylase releases glucose-1-phosphate from α-1,4-linked residues; the debranching enzyme handles branch points. Phosphoglucomutase converts glucose-1-phosphate to glucose-6-phosphate. In liver, glucose-6-phosphatase can produce free glucose for release into blood. In skeletal muscle, glucose-6-phosphate primarily enters glycolysis to help make ATP for contraction.
Study note: liver glycogen supports blood-glucose homeostasis; skeletal muscle glycogen supplies local fuel. Glycolysis does not directly require oxygen.
