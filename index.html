Negative feedback: opposes the original change and is the major mechanism for maintaining homeostasis.

Positive feedback: amplifies the original change and drives a process toward an endpoint.

Glucagon: pancreatic alpha cells; promotes hepatic glycogenolysis/gluconeogenesis during fasting.

Prolactin: milk production. Oxytocin: milk ejection and uterine contractions.
